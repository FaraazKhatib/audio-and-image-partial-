    What this can and cannot tell you
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Provenance is evidence. The models are estimates.
    
        
    
          A signed C2PA manifest or a leftover generation parameter block is direct proof, and it
          overrides everything else. The neural checks are statistical, and they are the part that
          can be wrong.
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Missing metadata means nothing.
    
        
    
          Messaging apps and most social platforms strip metadata from real
          recordings. An audio file with no history is not suspicious, so VeriTrust does not treat it
          as such.
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Recompression destroys the signal.
    
        
    
          These detectors read fine grained texture and frequency traces. Transcoding and aggressive audio compression erode exactly those traces, which pushes results toward
          uncertain.
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Newer generators are harder.
    
        
    
          A detector generalises best to the generators it was trained on. Output from models
          released after those checkpoints will be detected less reliably, and no score here should
          be read as a guarantee.
